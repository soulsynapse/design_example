# Phase Gate Review Checklists

Status: living document
Phases are defined in `PHASE_PLAN.md`. A phase closes when every item is checked or explicitly waived with a decision record, and the instructor accepts it. Record the achieved efficiency, key count, and cost per key at each gate in the "Measurements" table at the bottom so later values sit beside earlier ones.

## Gate 0: Brief approved
- [ ] `00-brief/project-brief.md` status approved; fixed requirements match `AGENTS.md`
- [ ] Stakeholder decision authority defined
- [ ] Constraints and assumptions registered
- [ ] Known unknowns transferred to `open-questions.md`

## Gate 1: Estudio previo complete
- [ ] Market study and comp set complete with cited sources
- [ ] Positioning statement approved
- [ ] Parcel planning classification confirmed; suelo rústico authorization route and DO status recorded
- [ ] Area schedule complete; every row has a basis; guestroom quantities sum to 150
- [ ] Room mix tied to segmentation; accessible count meets the stricter of DB-SUA and the regional reglamento
- [ ] Adjacency matrix complete including winery, spa, conference; harvest route separated from guest arrival
- [ ] Winery production defined
- [ ] Preliminary cost per key within brief envelope

## Gate 2: Anteproyecto approved
- [ ] At least three massing options scored against weighted criteria
- [ ] Selected option recorded as a decision
- [ ] Design narrative traces to positioning and brief; each principle rules something out
- [ ] Massing fits edificabilidad, height, and setbacks with stated margin
- [ ] 150 keys achieved at target efficiency on the outline plans
- [ ] Render viewpoint decided and recorded

## Gate 3: Proyecto básico part 1 (plans)
- [ ] Sheets A-101 to A-104 issued at 1:100 with north, scale, datum, room names, superficie útil
- [ ] A-002 fire plans: sectors, occupancy, evacuation lengths within DB-SI limits
- [ ] DB-SUA accessible routes and accessible rooms shown and dispersed
- [ ] Structural concept set; bays stack or transfers justified
- [ ] Plant locations and riser strategy on I-101
- [ ] Back-of-house and harvest flows traced on plans; crossings with guest paths justified or eliminated
- [ ] Plan takeoff reconciled to area schedule within tolerance
- [ ] Preliminary PEM within budget, or VE log opened
- [ ] Risk register reviewed

## Gate 4: Proyecto básico part 2
- [ ] A-201, A-301, A-401, A-402, A-501, A-601 issued
- [ ] Guestroom types meet tourism regulation minimums for the target category
- [ ] FF&E schedule and materials palette complete with performance requirements
- [ ] DB-HE compliance path documented
- [ ] Brand or operator standards table complete; waivers resolved
- [ ] Staffing model reconciled to employee facilities and seasonal winery labour
- [ ] PEM by capítulos within budget; pro forma meets stakeholder thresholds or gap recorded

## Gate 5: Deliverables accepted
- [ ] Final floor plans A-101 to A-104 complete per `STANDARDS.md` drawing conventions
- [ ] Rendered perspective R-01 meets the checklist in `05-architecture/rendered-perspective.md`
- [ ] Render shows nothing absent from the plans; materials match the palette
- [ ] All files status approved or explicitly marked out of scope
- [ ] Decision log complete, session notes present; no decisions exist only in meeting notes
- [ ] Open questions closed or formally handed over
