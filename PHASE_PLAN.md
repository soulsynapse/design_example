# Phase Plan

Status: draft — YYYY-MM-DD
Phases follow Spanish practice (LOE / CTE Anejo I stages) as defined in `STANDARDS.md`. Each phase names the files it produces and the exit criteria; the checklist that closes it is in `10-delivery/review-checklists.md`. Only the instructor closes a gate (see `AGENTS.md`).

The plan terminates at the two deliverables fixed in `AGENTS.md`: the floor plans and one rendered perspective. Every phase either produces an input those two need or is dropped.

## Phase 0 — Brief and setup
Purpose: make the owner's requirements explicit and establish the working rules.
- Produces: `00-brief/project-brief.md`, `00-brief/stakeholders.md`, `00-brief/constraints-and-assumptions.md`, `00-brief/glossary.md`; confirmation that `AGENTS.md` and `STANDARDS.md` are read and accepted.
- Exit: Gate 0. Brief approved; fixed requirements unchanged from `AGENTS.md`; known unknowns transferred to `10-delivery/open-questions.md`.
- Duration guide: one working session.

## Phase 1 — Estudio previo (market, site, program)
Purpose: establish what the land allows, what the market supports, and therefore how much of what the building must contain.
- Produces: all of `01-market/`; all of `02-site/` including the confirmed planning classification of the parcel and the suelo rústico authorization route; `03-program/space-program.md`, `area-schedule.csv` with every row given a basis, `room-mix.md` summing to 150 keys, `adjacency-matrix.md` including the winery and the harvest route; `08-operations/winery-operations.md` production definition; first-pass `09-financials/assumptions-register.md` and `pro-forma.md`.
- Key questions to settle: shared or separate kitchens for the two restaurants; conference capacity; spa scope; winery annual production and guest visibility; parking count.
- Exit: Gate 1. Area schedule complete and internally consistent; adjacency matrix has no unresolved X conflicts; preliminary cost per key within the brief envelope.
- Duration guide: three to four sessions. This is the longest phase because every later number depends on it.

## Phase 2 — Anteproyecto (concept and massing)
Purpose: choose the organizing idea and the massing that carries it on this site.
- Produces: `04-concept/design-narrative.md`, `massing-options.md` with at least three options scored, `precedents.md`; decision record for the selected massing in `10-delivery/decision-log.md`; first `assets/drawings/` massing diagrams; U-101 site plan at 1:500; outline floor plans at 1:200 showing the key count fitting the envelope; `05-architecture/rendered-perspective.md` viewpoint decided and a first massing-level view.
- Key questions to settle: how the guestroom wings sit relative to vine rows and views; whether the barrel cellar goes below grade; where harvest traffic enters relative to guest arrival; one building or a cluster.
- Exit: Gate 2. Massing fits the planning envelope with a stated margin; 150 keys achieved at the target efficiency; design narrative traces to positioning and brief.
- Duration guide: two to three sessions.

## Phase 3 — Proyecto básico, part 1 (plans)
Purpose: develop the selected massing into floor plans that resolve program, circulation, structure, and CTE basic requirements.
- Produces: `05-architecture/floor-plans.md` and sheets A-101 through A-104 at 1:100; `sections-and-elevations.md` with A-201 and A-301; `structural-concept.md` and E-101; `code-compliance.md` with DB-SI sectors and evacuation and DB-SUA accessible routes shown on A-002; `07-systems/mep-concept.md` riser strategy and plant locations on I-101; `08-operations/back-of-house-flows.md` traced on the plans; area reconciliation of plan takeoff against `area-schedule.csv`.
- Exit: Gate 3. All four plan sheets issued; evacuation lengths and sector sizes within DB-SI limits; accessible room count and dispersion met; no guest and service path crossings or each one justified; plan takeoff within tolerance of the area schedule; SD-level PEM within budget or a VE log opened.
- Duration guide: three to four sessions.

## Phase 4 — Proyecto básico, part 2 (envelope, interiors, systems, operations, finance)
Purpose: make the plans defensible from the outside in and from the operator's side.
- Produces: `05-architecture/facade-strategy.md` and A-501; `06-interiors/guestroom-types.md` with A-401 enlargements for every type including accessible variants, `public-areas.md`, `materials-palette.md`, `ffe-schedule.md`; `07-systems/` remaining files including `sustainability.md` DB-HE path; `08-operations/staffing-model.md`, `service-standards.md`, `brand-standards.md`; `09-financials/development-budget.md` as PEM by capítulos and updated `pro-forma.md`.
- Exit: Gate 4. Guestroom types meet the tourism regulation minimums for the target category; materials and facade match what the render will show; staffing reconciled to employee facilities; returns meet stakeholder thresholds or the gap is recorded.
- Duration guide: two to three sessions.

## Phase 5 — Deliverables and package
Purpose: produce the two deliverables at final quality and close the record.
- Produces: final floor plan sheets A-101 to A-104 (plus A-105 roof and A-601 area table as supporting sheets), fully titled per `STANDARDS.md`; the rendered perspective R-01 in `assets/renders/` meeting the checklist in `05-architecture/rendered-perspective.md`; every file in the tree at status approved or marked out of scope; `10-delivery/decision-log.md` complete; `open-questions.md` closed or handed over.
- Exit: Gate 5. Deliverables accepted by the instructor.
- Duration guide: two sessions, one for the render.

## Sequencing and dependencies
Phases run in order; within a phase the files may be worked in parallel. Two feedback loops are expected and are not failures: Phase 3 plans routinely send a change back to the area schedule (log it as a decision), and Phase 4 facade and material choices routinely alter the render viewpoint decided in Phase 2 (log that too). What is not allowed is a change to a Phase 0 fixed requirement without the owner.

## Milestones outside the design team's control
Confirmation of the parcel's planning classification; suelo rústico use authorization path; DO status of the parcel; target hotel category. Each is an open question until answered and each gates Phase 1 closure.
