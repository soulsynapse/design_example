## Purpose
A resort hotel of 150 keys set within a working vineyard in Soria province, Castilla y León, Spain, with a small on-site winery, a conference facility, a breakfast restaurant, a dinner restaurant, a bar, and a spa. The final deliverables are the floor plans and one rendered exterior perspective. Every later decision is checked against this paragraph.
