# Back-of-House Flows

Status: draft — YYYY-MM-DD
Flow diagrams in `assets/drawings/`. Adjacencies come from `03-program/adjacency-matrix.md`.

Trace each flow from entry point to end point and name every space it passes through. Flag any crossing of a guest path.

## Harvest and winery process
See `winery-operations.md`. Grape reception to dispatch, and the vehicle route it needs during vendimia. Must not share the guest arrival route.

## Receiving and food
Loading dock to receiving to dry, cold, and frozen storage to kitchen to outlets to dish return to waste.

## Linen
Soiled linen from guestroom floor to chute or cart to laundry (on-site or outsourced) to clean storage to floor linen rooms.

## Waste and recycling
Guestroom floors and kitchens to compactor and dock pickup.

## Luggage and group arrival
Porte-cochere to storage to room; group baggage pull.

## Housekeeping carts
Floor linen room to rooms; cart storage; service elevator queueing.

## Staff
Employee entrance, time clock, lockers, uniform issue, dining, to work areas.
