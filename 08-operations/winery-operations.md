A hotel is a building that is busiest every evening; a winery is a building that is busiest for a few weeks a year. The plan must let both happen on the same site without one degrading the other.

## Harvest flow
Vineyard to grape reception (vehicle access, weighing, sorting) to crush to fermentation to press to barrel to bottling to dry storage to dispatch. For each step: equipment, area, vehicle access, hours, noise, odour, effluent. Harvest traffic and hotel arrival must not share a route; record the resolution in `03-program/adjacency-matrix.md`.

## Guest interface
Which parts of the process guests see, from where, and when. Tasting room and shop adjacency to the bar or lobby. Tours during harvest raise safety and hygiene questions; record the policy here and the physical consequence (viewing gallery, separated circulation) in `05-architecture/floor-plans.md`.

## Environmental conditions
Barrel cellar temperature and humidity stability, which argues for below-grade or earth-sheltered construction; fermentation hall cooling load; process water and effluent treatment (DB-HS and the environmental authorization).

## Staffing and seasonality
Permanent winemaking staff and seasonal harvest labour; shared or separate employee facilities with the hotel.
