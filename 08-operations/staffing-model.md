## Departments and headcount
Front office, housekeeping, F&B (breakfast restaurant, dinner restaurant, bar), culinary, conference and banquets, spa, winery (permanent and seasonal harvest), engineering, sales and marketing, administration, security. For each: positions, FTE at stabilized occupancy, shift pattern.

## Peak concurrent staff
Drives locker count, employee dining size, and the service elevator load.

## Service model consequences
Full service vs. select service drives whether housekeeping is daily, whether there is room service, bell staff, concierge. Each affects the plan.
