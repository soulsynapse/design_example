## Program summary by zone
Describe each zone in prose. Totals are summed from the CSV; do not restate them here.

1. **Guestrooms and corridors.** 150 keys. Mix rationale in `room-mix.md`; typical bay width; corridor width; floor-level support. Resort guests stay longer and carry more; room and wardrobe sizes should reflect that.
2. **Arrival and lobby.** Arrival by car is near-certain in rural Soria; porte-cochere or covered drop-off, reception, luggage, lobby lounge, toilets.
3. **Food and beverage.** Breakfast restaurant sized for the morning peak at full occupancy; dinner restaurant sized for guests plus external diners, with the wine list as the draw; bar. Kitchen strategy (one shared kitchen serving both outlets with different service times, or two) is a decision to record in `10-delivery/decision-log.md` with its area consequence.
4. **Conference facility.** Main hall capacity and divisibility, breakouts, prefunction, storage and AV, its own service access and toilets. Capacity set from the market study.
5. **Spa.** Reception and relaxation, treatment rooms, thermal circuit and pool, fitness, changing. Scope set from positioning and the comp set.
6. **Winery.** Grape reception, fermentation, barrel cellar, bottling and dry goods, laboratory and office, tasting room and shop. Production parameters from `08-operations/winery-operations.md`. The cellar's environmental needs argue for below-grade or earth-sheltered placement.
7. **Back of house.** Receiving, housekeeping and laundry, employee facilities (hotel and seasonal winery staff), offices, engineering, storage, waste.
8. **Plant and distribution.** Thermal and electrical plant, IT rooms, cores and protected stairs per DB-SI.
9. **Site.** Parking, service yard, harvest vehicle route, outdoor amenity, relationship to vine rows. Areas in `02-site/site-analysis.md`.
