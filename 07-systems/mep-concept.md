## Mechanical
Under RITE and DB-HE. Guestroom conditioning options (fan coil, VRF, radiant with ventilation), central plant vs. distributed, outdoor air strategy, kitchen and laundry exhaust, spa and pool dehumidification, winery fermentation cooling and cellar conditioning as separate loads with a different seasonal profile. Heating dominates in Soria; chosen approach and reasoning.

## Plumbing
Domestic hot water generation with the DB-HE renewable contribution, recirculation, fuel, fixture flow rates, grease interception, winery process water and effluent treatment (see `08-operations/winery-operations.md`), storm management, water source if no municipal supply (well, storage).

## Electrical
Under REBT. Service size and utility coordination (rural supply may need a transformer centre), emergency and standby power scope, lighting controls, EV charging, harvest-period process loads.

## Riser and shaft strategy
Where the guestroom risers run relative to the bathroom back-to-back layout; shaft sizes and locations on the typical floor; main distribution routes at the ground and mechanical floors.
