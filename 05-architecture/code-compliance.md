# CTE Compliance Summary

Status: draft — YYYY-MM-DD
Governing regulation is named in `STANDARDS.md`; the planning envelope is in `02-site/zoning-and-code.md`. At proyecto básico level the CTE requires a justification of the basic requirements, not the full installation projects; this file is that justification in outline.

## DB-SI Seguridad en caso de incendio
- SI 1 Propagation inside: fire sectors by use (residencial público for guestrooms; pública concurrencia for restaurants, bar, conference; the winery as a separate industrial or storage sector), maximum sector areas, required resistance of separating elements, special-risk rooms (kitchens, plant rooms, laundry, barrel cellar).
- SI 2 Propagation outside: facade and roof separation between sectors and to the property line.
- SI 3 Evacuation: occupancy calculated per use, number and width of exits and stairs, maximum evacuation lengths, protected or specially protected stairs by height, evacuation of persons with disabilities (refuge zones, accessible routes).
- SI 4 Fire protection installations: extinguishers, hydrants, detection and alarm, sprinklers by height and area thresholds.
- SI 5 Fire brigade access: approach and manoeuvre space, relevant in a rural vineyard setting.
- SI 6 Structural fire resistance.
Fire safety plans are sheet A-002 in `drawing-list.md`.

## DB-SUA Seguridad de utilización y accesibilidad
- SUA 1 to 8 as applicable (falls, impact, entrapment, lighting, high-occupancy areas, drowning for the spa and any pool, vehicle movement, lightning).
- SUA 9 Accessibility: accessible route to every public and guest area, accessible guestroom count and dispersion (the stricter of DB-SUA and the Castilla y León reglamento), accessible toilets and changing rooms, signage. Show the accessible rooms on A-102 and A-401.

## DB-HE Ahorro de energía
Compliance path, envelope targets, primary energy limits, renewable contribution for domestic hot water; coordinated with `07-systems/sustainability.md`.

## DB-HR Protección frente al ruido
Airborne and impact insulation between guestrooms, between guestrooms and corridors, and between guestrooms and the bar, conference, plant, and winery spaces. Note the winery and kitchen as noise sources near guest areas.

## DB-HS Salubridad
Water supply, drainage, ventilation of guestrooms and bathrooms, damp protection, waste storage; the winery adds process drainage and effluent treatment.

## DB-SE Seguridad estructural
Reference to `structural-concept.md`; Eurocode basis.

## Hotel classification (Castilla y León tourism regulation)
Minimum guestroom and bathroom sizes, common-area requirements, and services for the target category; show the designed room against the minimum in `06-interiors/guestroom-types.md`.
